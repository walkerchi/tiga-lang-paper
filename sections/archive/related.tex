\section{Related work and positioning}
MLIR provides reusable infrastructure for retaining multiple abstraction levels
and implementing domain-specific compilers~\cite{mlir}. Tiga uses that approach
for relations, tensor operations and execution planning rather than proposing a
replacement compiler infrastructure.

Triton exposes tiled neural-network computation and compiles toward GPU
execution~\cite{triton}. In Tiga it is currently a downstream NVIDIA compilation
provider. The distinction is the level at which graph structure and reducer
algebra remain explicit, not a claim that a higher-level interface always wins.

PyTorch Geometric provides graph-learning abstractions and implementations built
around PyTorch~\cite{pyg}. Its optimized sparse paths reinforce the need to compare
matched operators rather than contrasting all graph frameworks with a naive
gather--scatter baseline. Tiga's present scope is compiler mechanisms, not a
comparable library of models, datasets and training infrastructure.

FlashAttention demonstrates the importance of IO-aware exact attention
implementation~\cite{flashattention}. Tiga's dense-relation and streaming-reducer
paths share relevant numerical structure. Its optional tile pruning changes the
mathematical contract and is described separately from exact attention. This
report does not claim a new attention algorithm or a general replacement for
specialized attention libraries.
